# 6. Addon Policy

Ultimate Warden encourages community-created Addons.

Creators may publish Addons for Ultimate Warden if the following conditions are met:

## 6.1 Dependency Requirement

An Addon must require the official Ultimate Warden datapack to function.

The dependency requirement must remain clearly stated on the Addon's distribution page.

The Addon must not contain a complete copy of Ultimate Warden.

## 6.2 File Distribution

An Addon may only contain:

- Original files created by the Addon creator.
- Files required for the Addon's functionality.
- Necessary integration files.

An Addon may include modified Ultimate Warden files only when those files are directly required for the Addon's functionality.

An Addon must not include unnecessary copies of Ultimate Warden files or assets.

## 6.3 Purpose Requirement

An Addon must extend or meaningfully expand Ultimate Warden.

Small changes alone do not automatically qualify as an Addon.

Examples of valid Addons:

- New bosses.
- New weapons.
- New dungeon expansions.
- Additional quests or story content.
- Compatibility improvements.
- Gameplay expansions.

Examples of invalid Addons:

- A copy of Ultimate Warden with renamed files.
- A version containing only small balance changes.
- A reupload with a different title.
